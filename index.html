Paver questions

Straight answers to what Ormond Beach homeowners ask most.

How much do pavers cost in Ormond Beach?

It depends on the size of the area, the paver type (concrete, travertine or brick), whether an old concrete slab has to be torn out, how much base work and grading is needed, and the pattern and borders. The fastest way to a real number is a free on-site quote, and it's worth comparing written quotes that list the same items.

Are pavers better than concrete in Florida?

Concrete costs less up front, but slabs crack as they settle and as roots push up, and cracks are hard to patch invisibly. Pavers cost more, but they flex instead of cracking, and a sunken or lifted section can be pulled up and relaid with the same pavers.

Travertine or concrete pavers for a pool deck?

Travertine stays cooler underfoot and has a natural, coastal look, which is why it's so common around Florida pools. Concrete pavers cost less and come in more colors. Lighter colors stay cooler in either material.

Can pavers be installed over my existing concrete?

Sometimes. If the slab is solid and level, thinner pavers or travertine can often be laid over it. If it's cracked, sinking or lifted by roots, it usually needs to come out first.

Do I need a permit or HOA approval?

Driveway aprons in the city right-of-way usually need approval from the City of Ormond Beach, and many Ormond neighborhoods have HOAs that approve colors and materials. Check both before work starts, and ask whether your contractor pulls any permits.

How often should pavers be sealed?

Sealing isn't required, but it helps lock the joint sand and resist stains and mold. Many Florida homeowners reseal every few years, depending on sun, shade and traffic.

How long does a paver project take?

Most residential patios, pool decks and driveways take a few days to about a week once work starts. Tear-out, walls, steps and rain can add time.
